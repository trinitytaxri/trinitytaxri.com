Services

Tax preparation

Individual federal and state returns. Self-employment (Schedule C) and rental property (Schedule E) returns, plus partnership and S-corp returns.

Bookkeeping

Monthly categorization and reconciliation, specializing in QuickBooks Online, with a monthly profit and loss and balance sheet. Your year-end books go straight into your tax return, so nothing gets lost in the handoff.

Tax planning

Quarterly estimated payments, S-corp election analysis, and a review of your qualified business income deduction. Useful any time of year, and most useful before December.

Not offered: payroll processing, sales tax filing, or audit and attest work. I'm happy to refer you to someone who does them.
